## Corrections to the supplied research

- Regulation 8 contains inspection and abatement conditions and documented exceptions. Floor area alone does not prove mandatory abatement, and building age alone does not establish an exemption.
- Jeffco's current guidance exempts interior-only work from WRC requirements. For certain siding projects, a permitted self-inspection/attestation route exists; it is not always a mandatory third-party specialist inspection. A reroof and a new building also have different defensible-space requirements.
- Golden building age is not equivalent to historic designation. The example hearing for one address cannot establish the process for every addition, or prove Summit participated in that project.
- The supplied Lakewood repeal story, permit-duration estimates, district counts, tax benefits, and current code editions were not used as verified facts. A current Lakewood official planning URL was resolved successfully; implementation uses it for address-specific verification.
- No language suggests that Summit inspected a particular house, photographed heave damage, completed a named project, or has specialist credentials without supporting evidence.
