## Case studies

Create a project page under `/our-projects/` when the owner supplies: an approved project name, service, city, actual scope, challenge, construction solution, outcome, and approved photographs. Capture consent for any address, name, testimonial, or budget that will be public. Avoid exact street addresses by default. Link the result to its city and service pages. No fabricated cases or stock images labeled as completed Summit work.

## Research follow-up

Priority investigations: official Lakewood zoning status; each city's building/energy code and effective date; Jeffco OWTS and well requirements for specific scopes; Golden's current local wildfire approach; live permit dashboards; utility capacity and provider-specific fees. Publish dated facts only after verifying the authoritative source and scope. Keep ADU, additional specialties, and new communities out of the service inventory until business scope is confirmed.

The supplied research's code trackers, tax incentives, contested housing legislation, and neighborhood hazard claims need an assigned maintenance owner before becoming public reference pages.

## Measurement

Before replacing the live site, obtain a Search Console baseline and review the generated redirect map. After launch, monitor indexation, broken URLs, organic clicks/impressions, and actual inquiry counts by service and city. Prioritize expansion using qualified inquiries and verified query demand. No search-volume or ranking claims were available during implementation.
